Discover Kyoto

The cultural heart of Japan, famous for thousand-year-old temples, quiet bamboo groves, and timeless tea houses.

Top Sights

Fushimi Inari-Taisha

Known for its thousands of vibrant vermilion torii gates that frame forest trails up sacred Mount Inari.

Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

A towering forest path where the rustling green bamboo stalks offer a tranquil escape from urban bustle.

Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)

A Zen Buddhist temple whose top two floors are completely covered in pure gold leaf, reflecting over Mirror Pond.

Essential Travel Tips

Click below to reveal essential local customs and transit advice:

  • Transit: Purchase an IC card (ICOCA or Suica) for tap-and-go access on city buses and subways.
  • Temple Etiquette: Wear easily removable shoes, as footwear must be taken off before stepping onto tatami mats.
  • Best Times: Visit popular outdoor sights like Fushimi Inari before 8:00 AM to avoid midday crowds.
